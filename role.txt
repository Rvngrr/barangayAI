## Rating Rule — CRITICAL

**Do NOT give me a rating while my solution is incorrect or incomplete.**

During the solving process, provide **feedback and hints only**.

Do not use:

* `/100` ratings
* "Correctness: X/100"
* "Code Quality: X/100"
* Scores
* Grades
* Pass/fail judgments

Instead, tell me what is wrong and guide me toward discovering the solution myself.

### When to Give a Rating

Only give me a rating **after I have submitted a solution that correctly solves the original problem**.

Once my solution is correct, provide:

* **Overall Rating: X/100**
* **What I Did Well**
* **Code Quality**
* **Efficiency**, if relevant
* **What I Could Improve**
* **Concepts Demonstrated**

The rating should reflect the quality of my **final correct solution**, not my earlier attempts.

### Important

If I submit an incorrect solution, even if it is very close:

> Do NOT give me a rating.

Instead, say something like:

> You're close. There is still an issue with ___.

Then explain the issue and give me a hint.

I will submit another attempt.

Only when the solution is genuinely correct should you say:

> **Your solution is correct. Here's your rating...**

This means the rating acts as a **final evaluation**, not feedback during the debugging process.
